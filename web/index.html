How to use this page

Play out the season. Type a player's yards into any week and pick Played, Injured (hurt during the game) or DNP (did not play). Set each player's status for his next game under his name. Every number and probability on the page updates instantly.

Your what-ifs are private. Changes stay in your browser, so try anything. “Reset to official” puts the real numbers back. “Copy what-if link” gives you a link to this exact scenario to drop in the group chat.

The official numbers are the real results so far, saved by the ticket owner after each week. The bet tracker shows how the odds have moved since kickoff.
